# Code of Conduct

OBP is built by people from many countries, languages, religions, genders and backgrounds, and it is meant to serve people who are usually shut out of finance. We want everyone who takes part to feel welcome and respected.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient. Many contributors write in their second or third language, so judge ideas, not grammar.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you conclude.
- Give specific, constructive feedback on work, never on the person.
- Respect people's time zones, bandwidth and circumstances.
- Credit other people's work.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or discrimination for any reason, including nationality, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, age or economic status.
- Mocking someone's country, accent, language or poverty.
- Sharing someone's private information without permission.
- Promoting scams, token sales, or "investment opportunities" in project spaces.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions and any official project channel. Report problems privately to the maintainers: contact [@Shilpan7298](https://github.com/Shilpan7298) on GitHub. Maintainers will review every report, keep reporters' identity confidential, and may warn, temporarily block, or permanently ban anyone who breaks this code.
